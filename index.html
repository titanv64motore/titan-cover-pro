Titan Cover Pro supports ISBN-13 and EAN-5 barcodes, with controls to position barcodes on the cover. The app description also highlights compact PDF files of around 89 KB and 130 KB, transparent PDF guidelines for text and cut positioning, and an app size of 5.60 MB.

It is described as an autonomous offline AI that evolves and learns, with fast operation even on older devices. The app is available through the Play Store for mobile phones and tablets, and can also be used on PC.
